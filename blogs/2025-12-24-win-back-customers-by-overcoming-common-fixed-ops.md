Matt Warpinski and Tiffani Stefanescu, Directors of Performance Management at Xtime Today there are more vehicles on the road than ever before, but the volume of dealer visits from 2018 to 2025 shows a significant drop. Service customers are going to independent repair shops and quick lubes instead of the dealership. And now we’re seeing new mobile services going out to people’s houses and doing work there.
